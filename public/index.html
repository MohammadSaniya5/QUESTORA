BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Your exam, clearly explained

  1. One attemptYou have one attempt. Your selected answers are saved as you progress.
  2. Question-based timingYou receive one minute for each question in your exam. The timer is controlled by the server.
  3. Answer carefullyChoose an option to lock your answer. You can move between questions before submitting.
  4. Earn rewardsEvery correct answer earns 10 coins. A five-answer streak earns a bonus.
  5. Review your resultAfter submission, check your score, accuracy, subject performance, coins and leaderboard position.
